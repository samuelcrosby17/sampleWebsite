// The Origin Story

Fairfield Campus Cart Co. was created to bridge the gap between busy campus schedules and quick, affordable access to refreshments. Recognizing that students and workers needed reliable options near high-density areas, our team combined retail operations expertise with mobile IT infrastructure to launch a high-performance food cart micro-business.
